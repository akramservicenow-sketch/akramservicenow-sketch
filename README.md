# Shaik Akram

**Senior ServiceNow Developer | IRM/GRC, ITSM, CMDB, Integrations | CSA · CAD**

I have spent 10 years building on ServiceNow and I'm based in Chicago. Most of my work is
taking over heavily customized instances and making them documented, upgrade-safe and
maintainable: finding what is really in use, replacing brittle customizations with platform
features where possible, and leaving behind something the next team can support.

My main focus areas are **IRM/GRC** and **Business Continuity Management**. I also do a lot of
ITSM and CMDB work, and integrations over **REST, SOAP and IntegrationHub** (including MID
Server for on-prem targets). Day to day that means a lot of **Flow Designer**, scripted
server-side logic and **Service Portal** work.
